## 3. Coefficient and Laurent-ring representation

For components in the already frozen order, let

```text
R = QQ[t_C, t_R0, ..., t_Rm]
L = QQ[t_C^+-1, t_R0^+-1, ..., t_Rm^+-1].
```

The computation is explicitly the scalar extension to `QQ` of the prior
integer-Laurent determinantal ideal. It does not detect integer torsion and must
not be reported as a reduced Gröbner basis over `ZZ`.

Laurent polynomials are represented internally as exact rational functions whose
denominator must be a monomial in component variables. Canonical Laurent output
is an ordered map from integer exponent tuples to reduced rational coefficients.

## 4. Complete determinantal generators

Let `A` be the frozen square Fox matrix, `r` its already certified fraction-field
rank, `R0` the lexicographically first independent `r`-row set, `C0` the
lexicographically first independent `r`-column set, and
`d = det(A[R0,C0])`.

Every rank-size minor is constructed. To avoid recomputing large determinants,
the exact rank-factorization identity is used:

```text
det(A[I,J]) = det(A[I,C0]) * det(A[R0,J]) / d
```

for every `r`-row subset `I` and `r`-column subset `J`. The implementation must:

- enumerate every subset in lexicographic order;
- compute the complete left and right maximal-minor families exactly;
- form every pair product through the identity;
- cancel exactly and reject a non-monomial denominator;
- normalize Laurent-unit associates before deduplication;
- record total, zero, nonzero, and deduplicated generator counts;
- directly recompute a frozen audit set of full minors and require exact equality.

The audit set is fixed before evaluation: first, last, and midpoint nonzero
row/column subset pairs; every pivot-neighbor obtained by replacing one pivot
index with the least available nonpivot index; and 32 pairs selected by SHA-256
ordering of the canonical subset-pair encoding. No random seed is used.

## 5. Monomial order, localization, and saturation

The polynomial-ring monomial order is pure lexicographic with:

```text
t_C > t_R0 > t_R1 > ... > t_Rm.
```

Laurent-unit localization is implemented by saturation. With
`T = product(component variables)`, introduce an auxiliary variable `z` and
compute in exact lex order

```text
z > t_C > t_R0 > ... > t_Rm
J = <polynomialized determinantal generators, 1 - z*T>.
```

The `z`-free elimination basis generates the saturated ideal
`I : T^infinity` in `R`. A second reduced lex Gröbner computation over `R`
canonicalizes that eliminated ideal. No saturation by `t_i - 1`, Alexander
factor, pivot minor, or empirically observed expression is allowed.

## 6. Canonical output

Each basis element is normalized to monic form over `QQ`. Terms use descending
lex order and exact reduced rational coefficients. The basis is sorted by
leading monomial and then its complete coefficient map. The receipt records:

- parent commit and presentation digest;
- backend and protocol identities;
- complete generator accounting and audit-minor results;
- pre-saturation generator digest;
- reduced saturated-basis coefficient maps and digest;
- leading monomials, total degrees, term counts, and variable support;
- elapsed time and peak resident memory;
- every failure or exhausted bound.

The protocol identity is the SHA-256 of the exact UTF-8 bytes of this committed
document. It is calculated only after this preregistration commit exists and is
then supplied as an immutable input to computation receipts.

## 8. Independent replay

The independent implementation may share only the frozen sparse presentation
and this protocol. It must not call `sympy.groebner` or reuse the primary basis.
It uses:

- a separately implemented exact sparse Gaussian elimination over rational
  functions to recover rank profiles and compound coordinates;
- explicit `fractions.Fraction` coefficient arithmetic;
- a deterministic Buchberger algorithm with the same lex order;
- independent interreduction to a monic reduced basis;
- direct mutual reduction of all primary and replay generators.

Replay passes only when:

1. complete generator-accounting counts and normalized generator digests agree;
2. each primary basis element reduces to zero by the replay basis and conversely;
3. the canonical reduced coefficient maps are byte-for-byte identical;
4. all frozen direct-minor audits pass independently.

Silence, matching dimension, matching generator count, or matching leading terms
alone is not agreement.

## 9. Evidence boundary

This protocol preserves the source diagrams, interval crossing certificate,
symbolic rank/onset evidence, and finite-character replay. It does not authorize:

- interpreting one pivot minor as a complete ideal generator;
- transferring the rational basis to an integer-torsion claim;
- selecting a phase law or separating phase co-winners after the fact;
- claiming ambient isotopy or complete link classification;
- prime forcing, arithmetic redefinition, spectral construction, zeta
  correspondence, or theorem status.

Length-four Milnor invariants and finite nilpotent quotients remain an independent
research route.
