## Definitions

**Origin** = unloaded distinction root before cardinal count or ordinal order.

**Arity** = count of mutually constraining roles required for a stable relation.

**Base arity primes** = `2,3,5,7`, the root arity carriers for this experiment.

**Invocation field** = simultaneous recursive activation of possible arity states from origin.

**Ordinal** = invocation-position within recursion; may be superpositional rather than scalar rank.

**Cardinal** = projected load-value carried by recursive arity state.

**Arity-load** = structured burden generated by recursive interaction among base arity carriers.

**Projection law** = rule Π declaring what survives collapse from invocation field to visible readout.

**Prime readout** = projected cardinal load that is prime.

**Prime epoch** = projected convergence event where arity-load becomes stable as a prime family-head readout.

**Composite family** = multiplicative descendant set generated by a prime family head.

**Composite shadow** = composite-family readout distinct from arity-transform offspring.

**Multiplicity of convergence** = number and diversity of independent arity-recursive structures converging on the same projected object.

**Zeta shadow field** = analytic aggregation of composite families generated by prime heads through the Euler product.

