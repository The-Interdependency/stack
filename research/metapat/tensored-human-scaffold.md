## Tensored Human and recursive social composition

The declared expression is:

$$
\mathcal H = V_H \otimes V_L \otimes V_S.
$$

The factors refer to the Human, Human Logistics, and Human Social structures above.
Their state spaces, coefficient types, and composition operation have not yet been
defined. This expression therefore carries declared structural intent, not an
implemented tensor algebra or a verified dimension count.

At social scale, each Tensored Human becomes a simplex vertex, enabling recursive
composition across scales. A representation must state what individual identity,
internal structure, relations, and state it preserves when a person participates
as a whole. The rule remains a declared requirement rather than a proven mapping.
