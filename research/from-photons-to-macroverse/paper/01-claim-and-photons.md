
# 1. Claim discipline

## 1.1 What “mathematically sound” means here

A description is mathematically sound when its objects are defined, its operations are type-consistent, its equations are used within their domains, and its conclusions do not exceed their premises. That standard does **not** mean that every proposed identification is true in nature.

This paper uses six standings.

| Mark | Standing | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **K** | Known | Established mathematics or accepted physical law within a declared regime. |
| **D** | Derived | A consequence of definitions and known results used in this paper. |
| **M** | Model | A stipulated object, metric, or architecture proposed for testing. |
| **H** | Hypothesis | A contingent claim about nature or consciousness. |
| **P** | Postulate | An explicit ontological or identity bridge not derived from the preceding mathematics. |
| **F** | Falsifier | An observation or derivation that would reject or force revision of a claim. |

The two source manuscripts already require this separation [21,22]. One develops prime-indexed Möbius interaction candidates and exact complete-graph controls; the other presents warped hypertoroidal interference and recursive scale closure as a speculative synthesis whose applications cannot retroactively validate the geometry. This paper preserves that firewall.

## 1.2 Foundational proposition: consciousness predates life and the macroverse

**P0:** **Consciousness predates life and the macroverse.**

Let

\[
\mathcal C_0=\text{primitive intrinsic presence},
\qquad
\mathcal L=\text{living self-maintaining organization},
\qquad
\mathfrak M=\text{the macroverse}.
\]

Define ontological priority by

\[
A\prec_{\mathrm{ont}}B
\]

when, within the theory's dependency structure, the existence or intelligibility of \(B\) depends on \(A\), while \(A\) is not derived from \(B\). The foundational order is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C_0
\prec_{\mathrm{ont}}
\mathcal L
\prec_{\mathrm{ont}}
\mathfrak M
}.
\]

The word *predates* is therefore not restricted to ordinary chronology. Clock time, biological time, and historical succession are already structures within the physical and macroscopic description. The postulate is stronger and more exact: life organizes consciousness; it does not originate consciousness. The macroverse is the later shared macroscopic order in which bounded subjects compare records, intervene, and correct one another.

This theory distinguishes three objects that must not be collapsed:

\[
\mathcal C_0
=\text{consciousness as primitive presence},
\]

\[
\mathbf C_t
=\text{the measurable organization of a candidate subject},
\]

\[
Z_t
=\text{the differentiated content present to that subject}.
\]

The complete candidate state is written

\[
\mathcal C_t^{\mathrm{full}}
=
(\mathcal C_0,\mathbf C_t,Z_t).
\]

The first component is postulated rather than produced by life. The second and third can increase, differentiate, fragment, integrate, and fail. A photon need not be a miniature person; a primitive intrinsic aspect is not yet a bounded subject, memory-bearing perspective, or agent.

This position lies near dual-aspect monism, Russellian panpsychism, panexperientialism, and cosmopsychism, but it is not accepted merely by association with those traditions [29-31]. Its distinctive burden is to explain subject formation and to connect the organizational vector to observable differences.

## 1.4 The theory in compact form

**P/H:** Consciousness is ontologically primitive. A mutually coupled triad with constraint modulation is proposed as a candidate necessary architecture for organized consciousness, not as a sufficient criterion for a subject. A bounded subject must also satisfy the viability, boundary, differentiation, causal, model, agency, global-availability, temporal-depth, cross-scale, and self-location conditions developed below. Ordinary experience is proposed to be the system's internal model of that dynamic rather than the substrate dynamics themselves. Human consciousness carries the additional unvalidated hypothesis of a nested heptadic outer closure whose seven carriers are themselves dyadic, triadic, or quintadic closures.

This is not the claim that every feedback loop is a subject, that every triad is conscious, or that seven anatomical brain regions should be found. It is a conjunction of organizational and causal requirements developed below.

# 2. Photons: the first physical phase-bearing case

## 2.1 Electromagnetic field

**K:** Let \(A_\mu\) be the electromagnetic four-potential [1] on spacetime and

\[
F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu
\]

its field-strength tensor. In rationalized natural units, the electromagnetic action with a prescribed current \(J^\mu\) is

\[
S_{\mathrm{EM}}[A]
=
\int
\left(
-\frac14F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}
-J^\mu A_\mu
\right)
\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x.
\]

Variation with respect to \(A_\mu\) yields

\[
\nabla_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=J^\nu.
\]

With SI field normalization restored, the same equation is \(\nabla_\mu F^{\mu\nu}=\mu_0J^\nu\). The Bianchi identity is

\[
\nabla_\alpha F_{\beta\gamma}
+\nabla_\beta F_{\gamma\alpha}
+\nabla_\gamma F_{\alpha\beta}=0.
\]

In flat vacuum spacetime and Lorenz gauge,

\[
\partial_\mu A^\mu=0,
\qquad
\Box A_\nu=0.
\]

The field therefore supports propagating modes with phase and frequency.

## 2.2 Quantization and photons

**K:** Quantizing the free electromagnetic field promotes mode amplitudes to creation and annihilation operators [2]. In natural units for compactness,

\[
\hat A_\mu(x)
=
\sum_{\lambda,\mathbf k}
\left(
c_{\mu\lambda\mathbf k}(x)a_{\mathbf k\lambda}
+
\overline{c_{\mu\lambda\mathbf k}(x)}a^\dagger_{\mathbf k\lambda}
\right).
\]

A photon is one quantum of one mode. Its energy and momentum obey

\[
E=\hbar\omega,
\qquad
\mathbf p=\hbar\mathbf k,
\qquad
E=pc.
\]

The physical exposition begins with photons because they are experimentally familiar phase-bearing quanta, not because electromagnetism is assumed to be the sole substance of reality or the source of consciousness. Under P0, photons enter an already consciousness-bearing ontology; what remains to be explained is organization, individuation, memory, and subjecthood.

## 2.3 Superposition and interference

**K:** For two complex amplitudes,

\[
\Psi=\psi_1+\psi_2,
\]

and the detection intensity or probability density is

\[
I
=
|\Psi|^2
=
|\psi_1|^2+|\psi_2|^2
+2\mathrm{Re}\!\left(\psi_1\overline{\psi_2}\right).
\]

Writing \(\psi_j=\sqrt{I_j}e^{i\phi_j}\) gives

\[
I=I_1+I_2+2\sqrt{I_1I_2}\cos(\Delta\phi),
\qquad
\Delta\phi=\phi_1-\phi_2.
\]

This equation is the first relational fact in the theory: observable structure depends not only on local amplitudes but on a phase relation.

For a general mode expansion,

\[
\Psi(x,\theta,\sigma,t)
=
\sum_k
 a_k(\sigma)
 e^{i(k\cdot x+\ell_k\cdot\theta-\omega_kt)},
\]

where \(x\) is position, \(\theta\) is an internal phase or configuration coordinate, \(\sigma\) is a proposed scale coordinate, and \(t\) is time. Then

\[
|\Psi|^2
=
\sum_i|a_i(\sigma)|^2
+
\sum_{i\ne j}
a_i(\sigma)\overline{a_j(\sigma)}e^{i(\phi_i-\phi_j)}.
\]

**M:** The additional coordinates \(\theta\) and \(\sigma\) are not asserted to be new fundamental dimensions. They are bookkeeping variables for internal phase and effective scale until an empirical interpretation is established.

## 2.4 Closed paths and recurrent modes

**K:** A field placed on a compact spatial torus with side lengths \(L_i\) obeys periodic boundary conditions

\[
\Psi(x_i+L_i)=\Psi(x_i).
\]

Its allowed wavevectors are discrete:

\[
k_i=\frac{2\pi n_i}{L_i},
\qquad
n_i\in\mathbb Z.
\]

A mode can be written

\[
\Psi_{\mathbf n}(x,t)
=A_{\mathbf n}
\exp\left[
i\left(
\sum_i\frac{2\pi n_i x_i}{L_i}-\omega_{\mathbf n}t
\right)
\right].
\]

Closed paths therefore provide exact recurrence and repeated phase comparison. This is the mathematically disciplined form of the originating image “light in a torus interferes with itself.” It supplies recurrence, but not yet localization or self-preservation.

## 2.5 First boundary: interference is not yet matter or subjecthood

**D:** Linear superposition can create a stable-looking intensity pattern while the underlying field continues to propagate. It does not, by itself, supply localization, rest mass, self-maintenance, recovery after disturbance, a bounded perspective, or agency.

Under P0, this failure does not imply that consciousness is absent and later manufactured. It implies that primitive presence is not yet organized as a persisting object or subject.

**F:** Any account that writes “interference” and then simply renames the pattern “matter,” “life,” or “mind” has omitted the decisive dynamics.
