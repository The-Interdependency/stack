# Intended PTCNA construction gate

**Authority:** Erin Spencer  
**Recorded:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** authority-declared architecture correction  
**Effect:** intended PTCNA construction is BLOCKED on upstream UCNS neural-audit evidence

## Correct dependency

PTCNA is derived, not filled in from architectural imagery.

```text
functioning conventional neural network
        ↓
UCNS audit
        ↓
source-bound relational evidence
        ↓
PTCNA architecture derived from that evidence
```

Until the UCNS audit exists, the intended PTCNA cannot be built.

The current repository contains a functioning PTCNA-shaped implementation, but it predates restoration of this prerequisite. It is therefore retained as a **historical pre-audit experimental scaffold**, not as the completed intended PTCNA.

## What may not be inferred

Absent audit evidence must remain unresolved. Do not infer layer counts, tensor sizes, prime/ring topology, propagation laws, weights, role partitions, or learning rules merely because an executable scaffold already contains them or because an architectural image suggests them.

Recovered prior source material or a new explicit authority statement may constrain these choices. Otherwise they remain `hmmm` until the UCNS audit supplies evidence from which a candidate can be derived.

## Language-input primitive

For the intended PTCNA language path, the primitive input object is a **UCNS Unicode-character gonol**.

No conventional tokenizer, token id, subword id, whole-string cryptographic fingerprint, or opaque external embedding vector may silently replace that primitive.

The exact way an audit-derived PTCNA composes or routes character gonols remains unresolved until the architecture is derived.

## Existing scaffold and falsification

The current `ptcna.experimental.v1` path projects whole input strings through a SHA-derived fixed-width feature representation and uses a preselected four-layer/ring scaffold. The frozen role-acquisition experiment that produced target accuracy `0.3333333333` versus `0.9444444444` for the hashed-linear fallback remains valid evidence about that exact scaffold and workload.

Do not erase, weaken, or rerun that result to repair its interpretation. Its referent is the pre-audit scaffold. It is not evidence that the not-yet-built intended PTCNA fails or succeeds.

## Next work

PTCNA-side implementation work is limited to preservation, provenance, and defining the future consumer boundary while UCNS performs the prerequisite neural audit.

After UCNS produces an exact audit-derived construction contract, PTCNA may build a new candidate from that evidence under a new identity and evaluate it under newly frozen criteria. The historical scaffold and its sealed result remain preserved.
