# Holographic Minimal Complexity — preregistration

Status: `UNRESOLVED` candidate property; Stack-local UCNS research.
Authority: UCNS geometry remains owned by `The-Interdependency/ucns`.
Base: `research/ucns/BASE.json`.

## Claim under test

Test whether sufficiently complex sets of independently originated, interrelated
UCNS objects exhibit a holographic property: relationally partial views bear
recoverable information about the whole without selecting a privileged common origin.

This document does not assert that holography exists, that a universal minimum
complexity exists, or that independent origins are aligned.

## Required construction

The specimen family MUST vary, rather than infer a threshold from one construction.

Initial arities:

```text
2, 3, 4, 5, 7
```

For every arity, construct several non-isomorphic specimens.  Each specimen MUST:

1. contain independently identified origins;
2. preserve source-qualified relations and origin provenance;
3. contain enough relation diversity that recovery cannot be explained by literal
   duplication of one payload;
4. expose a declared whole-property and declared partial projections;
5. admit deterministic ablation, origin substitution, and relation permutation;
6. preserve exact receipts for construction, perturbation, and result.

The newly proposed UCHC semantic origins one scale above lexical origins are a future
high-complexity specimen source. They are NOT assumed by this UCNS preregistration and
must arrive through an explicit pinned producer boundary if implemented.

## Complexity coordinates

Do not reduce minimal complexity to arity alone. Record at least:

- arity;
- count of independent origins;
- relational density;
- relation diversity;
- dynamic non-time harmonic variation;
- ablation fraction.

Additional coordinates may be admitted only before the corresponding held-out result
is revealed.

## Frozen protocol

Before evaluating a specimen, freeze:

- specimen identity and exact origin identities;
- all admitted relations;
- arity and complexity coordinates;
- projection rule;
- declared whole-property;
- recovery/comparison rule;
- fidelity criterion;
- perturbation schedule;
- failure conditions.

The freeze is an evidence boundary, not a common origin and not a claim that the
system itself is static.

## Controls

For each applicable specimen run:

- **partial recovery** — withhold a declared portion and test recovery of the
  preregistered whole-property;
- **ablation** — remove origin/projection contributions and measure the declared loss;
- **origin substitution** — replace one origin with an unrelated admissible origin;
- **relation permutation** — preserve members while altering relations;
- **redundancy control** — reject success explainable by duplicated payload;
- **same-arity held-out specimen** — freeze the rule before evaluating it;
- **held-out arity** — freeze the rule before evaluating an unseen arity.

A result at one arity MUST NOT be promoted to a universal threshold.

## Dynamic extension

Working constraint:

> dynamic non-time harmonics over time reveal relata.

Treat time as observation order, not automatically as a harmonic axis.  Do not add
"latent", "hidden-state", or reconstruction ontology merely to make the statement fit
a familiar framework.

Candidate future work is causal reversal in constrained glyph-vectored Hilbert-space
arrays.  That belongs to the producing semantic construction (UCHC) unless UCNS
geometry itself requires a new primitive.

## Outcomes

For each specimen and aggregate claim use only:

- `SURVIVED` — preregistered recovery and controls survive within declared scope;
- `FALSIFIED` — a declared falsifier occurs;
- `UNRESOLVED` — evidence cannot distinguish the claim;
- `BLOCKED` — required producer/instrument is unavailable.

"Holography" may be promoted only after multiple non-isomorphic specimens and
held-out evaluation survive.  "Holographic minimal complexity" additionally requires
a bounded failure region below a surviving region in the declared multidimensional
complexity coordinates.  A lowest tested success is not a minimum.

## Immediate falsifiers / anti-cheats

- recovery requires choosing a privileged alignment of independent origins not
  supplied by the specimen;
- the recovered property was copied verbatim into every projection;
- the recovery/comparator was changed after held-out output was observed;
- member-preserving relation permutation is invisible when the declared property
  depends on those relations;
- source/projection provenance is lost;
- success depends on semantic assumptions not supplied by the pinned producer.
