`english.ordinal/1` is the selected construction. Inspect it with
`PYTHONPATH=research/english-gonol python -m english_gonol profile`.
Run `verify OUTPUT` before consuming an artifact. These boundaries do not reopen

## Construction law

For zero-based occurrence i among n participants, radius is R/2**(n-i); the newest
is at R/2. Angle is the participant's exact index/arity on its declared carrier.
Closed interiors remain recoverable through recursive atomic participation.
The initial equidistant field and subsequent circular placement are distinct;
the latter is not claimed to preserve every initial pairwise distance.

This profile uses balanced insertion around inherited scalar anchors and orders
its word carrier by public-gonol-index sequences. These are explicit versioned
layout choices, not discovered semantic laws. Changing them requires rebuilding.

### 4. Prior definitions and applicable functions: reconstructed on the new base

Operator answer: `define them the same way the previous iteration did. most of
that part of the previous implementation should still apply, just requires
iteration over the new base.`

`../english_gonol/language/character_definitions.py` and its
`data/character_definitions_v1.json` remain the unchanged definition/behavior
sources. The replacement `../english_gonol/ordinal_definitions.py` iterates the
expanded carrier, retaining every inherited entry, overlapping role, pronunciation,
orthographic rule, number-name link, numerology entry and explicit unknown.
It calls UCNS ordinal affixiation, never the historical constructor. Verified
entries reuse the prior final-y behavior rather than duplicate its law.

The supported `build` now requires `character-definitions.json`. Its digest is
mandatory in the geometry manifest; `verify` replays every definition placement,
ordered interior, source binding and carrier index by an independent formula.
Missing, extra, reordered or altered data fail. Existing artifacts must be rebuilt;
old receipts are not rebranded as evidence for the changed implementation.

The committed `../experiments/ordinal-definitions/migration-witness.json` records
reconstruction over all 4,698 scalars from the supplied complete field: 4,757
definition closures and ten number-name interiors matching existing corpus word
receipts. The inherited source leaves 4,643 scalar definitions explicitly unknown.
This is full definition-layer coverage, not a new complete-corpus admission replay
or current-head hosted CI. `../tests/test_ordinal_definitions.py` supplies
preservation, no-old-constructor, independent-replay and corruption tests.

### 5. Vocabulary growth: open

Current behavior is an exact pinned repertoire. Queries reject unknown words and
unadmitted scalars, without normalization or dynamic carrier mutation. Changing
carrier order or arity requires a new profile and rebuilding affected placements.
An open-text admission policy must preserve old-profile replay and avoid silent
reinterpretation; neither last-glyph angles nor invented hash angles substitute
for that policy. This question does not block the existing pinned construction.
